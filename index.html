🧥 What to Pack ▼
🧥 Clothing (May = Unpredictable)
🧥
Waterproof shell jacket Wind + rain + snow possible every day · Non-negotiable
🧤
Gloves + warm hat + fleece layer Nighttime temps can drop below freezing · Summit of Washburn ~35°F
👖
Waterproof pants or rain pants Snowmelt makes trails muddy · Especially Fairy Falls, Taggart Lake
👟
Waterproof hiking boots or trail runners Mud season peaks in May · Regular sneakers will be soaked within 30 min
☀️ Sun & Safety
🧴
SPF 50+ sunscreen + SPF lip balm High elevation = intense UV even on cloudy days · Reapply every 2 hrs
🕶️
Polarized sunglasses Snow glare + bright skies · Essential on Washburn summit
🦟
DEET insect repellent Mosquitoes peak in May especially near thermal areas and lakeshores
🎒 Gear & Essentials
🎒
Daypack (20–25L) For layers, water, snacks, sunscreen · Every hike needs one
💧
Reusable water bottle (2L+) High altitude dehydrates fast · Refill at visitor centers
🔭
Binoculars Bears, wolves, bison, moose — all more visible from distance. 8×42 minimum
🍫
Snacks / trail food Limited food options mid-park · Pack lunch for Days 2 & 3 from hotel in the morning
🔋
Portable charger / power bank Long days with GPS, photos, NPS app · Dead phone = no maps in dead zones
💊
Ibuprofen / altitude headache meds Elevations hit 8,000–9,000 ft · Headaches common first day · Stay hydrated
